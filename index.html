CPA RAG Assistant — Document-Grounded Q&A

In progress

A retrieval-augmented assistant for a CPA firm's internal documents, built around the part that actually matters for a firm to trust it: guardrails, access control, and citations — not retrieval alone.

  • ChromaDB (embedded vector store)
  • Custom chunking / retrieval pipeline
  • Claude or Groq (togglable LLM)
  • Role-based access filtering
  • pytest

Answers questions grounded in a firm's own documents with citations back to the source, refuses to answer when retrieval confidence is too low instead of guessing, and filters retrieved content by the asking user's role.
